# Provenance and Licensing

colbertcache does not download or publish datasets. It helps you make local demo fixtures auditable.

For every fixture mirror, document:

- where the source data came from;
- who owns or licenses it;
- whether the fixture is synthetic, transformed, sampled, or copied;
- what demos may safely use it;
- any attribution required by upstream material.

The bundled sample fixture is synthetic and MIT licensed.
